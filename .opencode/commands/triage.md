---
description: Look up a known issue without fixing it
---

Read the Quick triage table at the top of `KNOWN_ISSUES.md`, then the matching entry for $ARGUMENTS. If $ARGUMENTS is empty, show the Quick triage table.

Report: KI ID, status, one-line symptom, files that carry it, and whether it is safe to touch.

Do not fix an open KI unless the user asks. Cite the KI ID in any later change. Point to the relevant `docs/AGENT_OPS.md` section instead of restating the whole document.
