Guided calibration: collect, measure, fit, compare.

Four steps, one robot. Two tiers: Express (no tape, wall-anchored dynamics in minutes) and Lab (the full tape suite for odometry, drift and slip). Everything lands in calibration/<name>.*: the full hub session (.session.csv, every DATA row and META line kept), your tape numbers (.sheet.csv, weigh-in included), the poses (.poses.csv) and the fit (.sysid_fit.json, intervals included). A dropped Bluetooth tail is flagged, never silently cut. Pushed trajectory sessions are compare-only and are never fitted here.

2. Measure

    
The measurements the session needs, as a form.
      Every measurement is in the frame the robot sat in at the start of the test:
      x_mm forward from the start pose, y_mm to the left of the runway,
      turn_right_deg to the right. Measure from the robot's centre, not its bumper.
      A test that does not turn has a turn of 0.

    

    
The weigh-in: the robot's mass with the battery in,
      on a kitchen scale, three times.

    

    

    

      
      
    

  


  
  

    
3. Fit

    
The session and the sheet become the measured model.

    
Express needs no tape: wall_homing is the distance anchor, so give
      the wall distance when you have a wall. Lab adds the tape rows on top.

Before: the prior fit, or the nominal robot config when there is none.
